RAPID GROWTH Model

Eleven inputs that help Ada tune difficulty, tone, and pacing—so every learner feels seen.

R — Resume & Academic History

Background, strengths, gaps → skill vectors & adaptive pacing from day one.

A — Avatar Photo & Sentiment Baseline

Mood trends so tone matches emotion in real time.

P — Learning Preferences

Visual, auditory, hands-on, solo/collab → right format beyond text.

I — Interests & Hobbies

Motivation hooks (dinosaurs, Star Trek) for sticky analogies.

D — Difficult Subjects

Confidence ratings direct scaffolding and practice.

G — Goals & Milestones

Backward-chains from dream goals to today’s plan.

R² — Routine & Availability

Preferred time blocks → timely nudges & spaced review.

O — Obstacles / Accommodations

ADHD, dyslexia, bandwidth → UI and pacing adapt.

W — Well-Being Pulse

5-item check-ins adjust challenge and encouragement.

T — Tech Comfort & Tools

Device readiness unlocks immersive features.

H — Historical Performance Data

Grades & scores cold-start the right level.
